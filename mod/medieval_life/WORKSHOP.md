# KCD2 Life

**Henry goes back to Rattay. You stay, and live a life of your own in medieval Bohemia.**

Henry goes back to Rattay, and you stay behind as a nobody in the Trosky region: a new man or woman with a name, a family and a few groschen. Find work, keep yourself fed, build a good name, and stay on the right side of the law.

*Requires KCD2 Female Framework and KCD2 Male Framework.*

## A new life

- **A new start.** In Troskowitz, a wayfarer by the tavern wall offers you a new life, from 08:00 to 20:00.
- **A few words to start.** Short notes tell you how to get by.
- **Who you are.** Choose a man or a woman, and look through the faces of the common folk: Czech, Hungarian or Romani. Type a name, or draw one from the names of the time and of that people.
- **From scratch.** Your attributes start at 0, your skills at 1, and you know no recipes. Your family gives you clothes, a weapon and money:
  - a peasant family: a mace and 15 groschen;
  - an innkeeper family: a hunting sword, 40 groschen and a job at the tavern;
  - a burgher family: a sword and 300 groschen.
- **A fresh map.** The places Henry found are hidden again, and so are his beds and homes on the map. You have no dog, though Mutt shows in your inventory.
- **Your own calendar.** Your life starts on 1 June 1403. A month lasts a week of work, or 14 days, or the real calendar if you use Mod Configuration Menu.

## Work

- **Day work** in every town and village of the Trosky region. Talk to the owner or the foreman in working hours, mostly 08:00 to 18:00, and choose your hours. Some keep shorter ones, and they tell you when. Each hour pays, trains the skills of the trade, and makes you hungry and tired.
- **A steady job**, with a shift every day, 6 days on and 1 off, a meal, and a bed by your workplace. Come late two days in a row, miss a shift, or turn up drunk or naked, and that is a fault. Three faults and you are out.
- **The book of trades** keeps your record: your name, birth date, parents, and where you worked and for how long. It also shows the hours of the businesses you know nearby, and when their next errands come. Lose it, and a new one starts empty.
- **A bed for the night,** or for a week at the price of five nights, at the taverns, the inn and the other places that rent one: ask the owner, and the map shows you where it is. Nobody minds you by it while it is yours. The game's own offer to rent a bed is Henry's.
- **Every bed saves the game,** even a stranger's.
- **A stash of your own.** Your chests are yours, not Henry's, also those of The KCD2 Camping Mod and Mercenaries. Henry's stash waits for him.

## Errands

Every business asks for errands on its own day of the week, never farther than the next town. Each one shows in your journal with a marker on the map, and a paper slip says what is due.

- **Deliveries:** take goods to someone.
- **Pickups:** fetch an order and bring it back.
- **Shopping:** take the money, buy the goods, and keep what you save.
- **A good name opens doors.** Pickups and shopping come after you have proven yourself, with deliveries or with hours of work for that business. Each failure makes the business trust you less.
- **Your employer has errands too,** like any other business.
- **Businesses keep their hours:** errands are given and delivered only while they are open, and the owner tells you when an errand can wait until tomorrow.
- **Fail an errand** and you owe the value of the goods, and that business gives you no work until you pay.
- **Steal too much** and the guards come for you: three unpaid slips in one town, and they will be waiting.
- **Too hard?** Mod Configuration Menu changes the time, pay and frequency of errands, the trust and reputation they need, what a failure costs and when the guards come.

## Good to know

- **From the pillory on.** The new life is there once Henry has been through the pillory in Troskowitz, early in the story. You do not need to finish the game.
- **Any save will do.** Add it to the save you are playing: no new game needed. Henry's alchemy recipes are hidden while the mod is installed, except the two he needs in the prologue. They come back when you remove the mods.
- **In a new game,** Henry's playthrough works as usual.
- **Keep a save from before the new life.** It is the safest way back to Henry.
- **Kuttenberg** opens to you once Henry's story reaches it.
- Mods that change the game's own alchemy recipes can clash with it. Mods that add new potions, like ldpotions, work.

## Played with other mods

KCD2 Life has been played together with 47 other mods at once. These make a new life better:

- **Mercenaries:** hire your own company and make camp with them. Start with nothing, earn your groschen, and one day lead men of your own.
- **The KCD2 Camping Mod:** pitch a camp wherever you are. A nobody with no home always has a bed, and every bed saves the game.
- **Realish Economy:** prices and money closer to the real thing. Your wages and errands follow it, so every groschen you earn counts.
- **The Nutrition Mod:** fruit, meat, vegetables and water, not just a full belly. A working life with meals to plan, and your hours of work count.
- **The KCD2 Cooking Mod:** cook your own meals. Buy the food with your wages and feed yourself.
- **Realish Wounds and Realish Crime:** wounds and the law closer to the real thing, so a fight or a theft costs a nobody dearly.
- **ldpotions:** more potions to brew, and more recipes for your new character to learn.
- **Mod Configuration Menu:** settings for the calendar, work, errands and voice.

The full list is on [GitHub](https://github.com/vaiciant/kcd2-life/blob/main/mod/medieval_life/TESTED_MODS.md). Others should work too.

## Playing Henry again

Type `ml_switch yes` in the console to switch between Henry and your new character. Your new life waits while Henry plays. Use it with care: switching can break your KCD2 Life save.

## Removing it

Go to the Trosky region and type `ml_uninstall yes` in the console. It closes the mod's quests, your job and your errands, gives the game back to Henry with Mutt, and saves. To remove the mods, quit and take them out: Henry gets his recipes back.

## Installing

Subscribe to the three mods, or download them from Nexus Mods and put their folders in the game's `Mods` folder. That is all: no launch options and no tools.

Do it when Henry is ready to hand over: save next to the wayfarer by the tavern wall in Troskowitz, between 08:00 and 20:00, install, load and talk to him.
