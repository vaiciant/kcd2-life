# KCD2 Life: mods it has been played with

KCD2 Life, with KCD2 Female Framework and KCD2 Male Framework, has been played together with all of these at once, on *Kingdom Come: Deliverance II* 1.5.6. A new life, work, errands, saving and loading went on without errors. Other mods should work too: these are the ones tested.

Some of them change the same systems as KCD2 Life, and that is expected, not a fault:

- **Realish Economy** makes prices about a quarter. KCD2 Life finds it and pays and charges a quarter too, and so does the women's barber of KCD2 Female Framework.
- **The KCD2 Camping Mod** and **Mercenaries** have beds with no owner, which serve whoever plays, and chests that open the player's stash. In the Trosky region, that is the stash of whoever plays: Henry or the new character.
- **Mercenaries:** starting a new life, `ml_switch` and `ml_uninstall` pay off the whole company first, as `merc_dismiss` does.
- **ldpotions** and **ERVWHDO** add recipes with a table patch, on top of the recipe table of KCD2 Life: the new character learns them as Henry would.
- **enhancedbohemiancreatures** adds beards and hair to the barber's list that only fit Henry's head. The new character, and Henry with a look of KCD2 Male Framework, choose in a talk among what fits the face, and Henry with his own face keeps the game's barber with those of EBCO.
- **checkerealtime** runs the clock in real time, so a working day of KCD2 Life lasts real hours.
- The mods of food, wounds and crime change hunger, damage and the law for the new character as they do for Henry. With **The Nutrition Mod**, the hours of work use up food and drink, as time would.
- **Immersive Eating** plays an animation when an item is used, and reading a document counts. KCD2 Life adds its book of trades and its errand slips to the documents that mod leaves alone, as the game's books are: reading them plays nothing.
